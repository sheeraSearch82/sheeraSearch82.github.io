I write machine-checked proofs that real systems do what they claim.

I am an Assistant Professor at TKM College of Engineering, Kollam. My PhD at IIT Madras built a mechanically verified garbage collector for OCaml.

I work in F*, Pulse and separation logic. With the F* team at Microsoft Research and FP Launchpad at IIT Madras, I am building a verified garbage collector in Pulse. The RiSE blog at Microsoft Research tells how it grew from my thesis.
